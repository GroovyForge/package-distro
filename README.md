## Cored

Cored is a free Apple Music player for Android TV, with optional support through Buy me a coffee via the qr code below, also available in the app's About section.

I'm testing demand before committing to an app-store release. My main concern is protecting my family's privacy, I'm not comfortable publishing our home address on a store listing. Registering a business, virtual address, etc all add to the ongoing costs, alongside the Apple Developer subscription and maintaining the app.

I started this after constant frustration not being able to play apple music natively on the ShieldTV, casting from the phone is just an annoyance.

GitHub downloads let me gauge interest before taking on those extra costs. I understand that sideloading isn't for everyone, including people who would only use Cored through Google Play.

See [Cored](cored/) for release and update information. Cored is not currently available for download here. In-app update checks are planned, but are not available yet.
