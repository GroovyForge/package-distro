## Cored

Cored is a free Apple Music player for Android TV, with optional support through Buy me a coffee in the app's About section.

I'm testing demand before committing to an app-store release. My main concern is protecting my family's privacy. I'm not comfortable publishing our home address on a store listing, so I'd use a business address. That adds to the ongoing costs, alongside the Apple Developer subscription and running the app.

GitHub downloads let me gauge interest before taking on those extra costs. I understand that sideloading isn't for everyone, including people who would only use Cored through Google Play.

See [Cored](cored/) for release and update information. Cored is not currently available for download here. In-app update checks are planned, but are not available yet.
